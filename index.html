About Shri Krishna

A timeless presence whose stories blend devotion, wisdom, compassion, courage and joy.

The All-Attractive One

Shri Krishna is one of the most revered figures in Hindu tradition. His life and teachings are remembered through the stories of Mathura, Gokul, Vrindavan and Kurukshetra.

From the playful childhood of Gokul to the profound wisdom of the Bhagavad Gita, Krishna's narrative brings together devotion, responsibility, love and dharma.

Janmashtami celebrates his divine birth and invites devotees to remember the values associated with his life: compassion, courage, wisdom and unwavering devotion.

Let every moment become an opportunity to remember the divine, serve with sincerity, and walk the path of dharma.

Divine Stories

Krishna Leelas

Stories from Krishna's life that continue to inspire generations.

👶

Divine Birth

Krishna was born to Devaki and Vasudeva in Mathura under extraordinary circumstances. His birth is celebrated as a triumph of divine purpose over darkness.

🧈

Makhan Chor

The playful stories of young Krishna stealing butter became beloved symbols of his sweetness, innocence and intimate relationship with the people of Gokul.

🪈

Divine Flute

Krishna's flute is one of his most recognizable symbols. Its music is traditionally described as enchanting the beings of Vrindavan.

💃

Raas Leela

The Raas Leela represents a profound devotional relationship between Krishna and the gopis and is celebrated as an expression of divine love.

⛰️

Govardhan Leela

Krishna is traditionally remembered for lifting Govardhan Hill to protect the people of Vraja from torrential rain.

🏹

Victory Over Kansa

Krishna ultimately defeated Kansa, ending his tyranny and restoring hope to Mathura.
